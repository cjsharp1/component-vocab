Footer

The bottom-level site footer containing secondary navigation, legal information, contact details, and social links. Persists across pages.

Also known as
In design systems: GOV.UK footer (GOV.UK)

Layout Variations

Usage Notes

  • Place as the last child of <body>.
  • Always include copyright/legal text.
  • Use Grid inside Footer for multi-column layouts.
