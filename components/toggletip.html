Toggletip

A small info button that shows a short explanation when pressed and hides it again on a second press, Escape or a click elsewhere. Use it instead of a Tooltip when the button exists only to reveal the explanation, or where hover isn't available.

Also known as
In design systems: Toggletip (Carbon); Toggle Tip (Chakra UI)
Semantic element
<button> plus a live region with role="status"
Research note
A tooltip opened by click. Keeping the name because it behaves differently for assistive tech

Usage Notes

  • Use a Toggletip when the explanation should appear on a press. Use a Tooltip for a short label shown on hover and focus, and a Popover when the content includes links or controls.
  • Make the trigger a <button> with an accessible name. Put the message in a live region such as role="status" so screen readers announce it when it appears.
  • Close it on Escape, on a second press of the trigger and on a click outside, and leave focus on the trigger.
  • Keep the content to a sentence or two. Anything people need to complete a task belongs on the page, not behind an icon.
  • Give the trigger a target of at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels (WCAG 2.5.8 Target Size, Minimum).
