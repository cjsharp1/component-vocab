Banner

A narrow, high-visibility strip used for announcements, promotions, or time-sensitive alerts. Typically spans full width at the top or bottom of a page.

Also known as
Alert (Bootstrap, partial), Banner (Material), Alert (Ant, partial), — (Apple), — (shadcn)

Usage Notes

  • Use for site-wide announcements. For user-action feedback, use Alert. For temporary notifications, use Toast.
  • Keep Banner messages concise. Users may dismiss them quickly.
  • Avoid stacking multiple banners — prioritize the most important message.
  • Use appropriate ARIA roles: role="banner" for general announcements, role="alert" for urgent messages.
