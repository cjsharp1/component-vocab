Showcase

A focused, detailed presentation of a single product, project, or work sample. More detailed than a Card — shows the item with full context rather than as a teaser.

Usage Notes

  • Use for portfolio items, case studies, or product detail sections. For teasers that link elsewhere, use Card. For side-by-side comparisons, use Comparison.
  • Include high-quality images or screenshots — this is the primary visual.
  • Descriptions should be more detailed than Card excerpts — explain the work in full.
  • Metadata tags (tech stack, date, etc.) help users understand context.
