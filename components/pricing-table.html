Pricing table

Pricing plans or tiers presented for comparison and selection. Shows plan names, prices, features, and purchase actions side by side.

Layout Variations

Usage Notes

  • Use for subscription/plan comparison.
  • For general feature comparison (not pricing), use the comparison variant of Table.
  • Highlight the recommended plan to guide user choice.
  • Keep feature lists consistent across plans — show what's included and what's not.
