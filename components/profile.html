Profile

A person or team member presentation. Shows photo, name, role, and optional bio and social links. Used for team pages, author bios, and speaker lists.

Usage Notes

  • Use for team pages, speaker lists, or author attribution. For a small inline avatar (e.g., in a comment), use Avatar instead.
  • Photos should be consistent in size and aspect ratio within a collection.
  • For accessibility, always include alt text on profile photos describing the person.
  • Social links should use recognizable icons with appropriate aria-label attributes.
