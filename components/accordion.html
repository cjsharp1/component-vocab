Accordion

Expandable/collapsible content panels. Each panel has a header that reveals or hides associated content when triggered.

Also known as
Collapse, Expander, Details, Collapsible. In design systems: Accordion (MUI, Bootstrap, Carbon, Chakra UI, shadcn/ui, Fluent UI, GOV.UK); Collapse (Ant Design); Disclosure controls (Apple HIG)

Usage Notes

  • Use for FAQs or content that benefits from progressive disclosure.
  • For tabbed content (switch between panels), use Tabs.
  • Don't nest Accordions.
  • Keep panel headers short and descriptive — they should clearly indicate what content is hidden.
