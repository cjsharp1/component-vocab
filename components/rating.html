Rating

Shows a score as a row of stars or similar symbols, or lets people choose one. Use the display version for averages and reviews, and the input version in review forms.

Also known as
In design systems: Rating (MUI, Chakra UI, Fluent UI); Rate (Ant Design); Rating indicators (Apple HIG)
Semantic element
Display: text with a text alternative. Input: <fieldset> with radio inputs
Category
G. Feedback and status

Usage Notes

  • There is no native rating element. For display, give the whole rating one text alternative, such as visually hidden text "4 out of 5 stars", and hide the individual symbols with aria-hidden="true". An aria-label on a plain <p> or <span> isn't reliably announced.
  • For input, use a <fieldset> with a <legend> and one radio input per score, each labeled like "3 stars". Arrow keys and screen readers then work without extra scripting.
  • Filled and empty stars must differ by more than color, such as solid versus outline shapes (WCAG 1.4.1 Use of Color). Showing the number as text helps too.
  • Show the scale ("out of 5") and, where it matters, the number of ratings, so a perfect score from two people isn't read as strong evidence.
  • In the input version, make each star at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels (WCAG 2.5.8 Target Size, Minimum).
