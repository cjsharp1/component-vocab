Date input

A typed date, entered in a native <input type="date"> or in separate day, month and year fields. Use it for dates people already know, such as a date of birth; use a Date picker when choosing from a calendar helps.

Usage Notes

  • Use a Date input for dates people know without looking them up, such as a birth date or a card expiry date. Typing them is faster than paging back through a calendar.
  • With separate fields, wrap them in a <fieldset> whose <legend> asks the question, label each field (Day, Month, Year), and use inputmode="numeric" rather than type="number".
  • Show the expected format in a hint linked with aria-describedby, and size each field to its content so the widths suggest the format.
  • Add autocomplete values where they apply, such as bday-day, bday-month and bday-year, so browsers can fill them in (WCAG 1.3.5).
  • Native type="date" looks and behaves differently across browsers and follows the user's locale format. Test it with your audience before relying on it.
