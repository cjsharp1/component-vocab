Usage Notes

  • Use for key metrics that need emphasis. Group in Stats (collection) for impact. For detailed data, use Table.
  • Keep labels short and descriptive — 1-3 words.
  • Format large numbers for readability (e.g., 1.5M instead of 1,500,000).
  • Trend indicators should be meaningful — show percentage change and time period.
