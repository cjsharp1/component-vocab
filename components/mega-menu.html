Mega menu

A large panel of grouped links that opens from a top-level item in the site Navigation, for sections with too many pages for a short list. It is built from disclosure buttons and lists of links, not from an ARIA menu.

Usage Notes

  • Open each panel with a <button aria-expanded aria-controls>, as in the ARIA Authoring Practices disclosure navigation example. Don't use role="menu": it expects application-style arrow-key behavior and changes how the links are announced.
  • Panels must open on click, Enter and Space, and close with Escape, returning focus to the button. If they also open on hover, keep them open while the pointer moves into the panel (WCAG 1.4.13).
  • Give each link group a heading and mark the links up as a list, so screen reader users can skim the panel's structure.
  • If the top-level item also has its own page, add a link to it inside the panel, such as "All products". Don't make the button both open the panel and navigate.
  • On small screens, show the same groups as stacked Disclosures, often inside a Drawer.
