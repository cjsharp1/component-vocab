Icon

A small symbol, usually an inline <svg>, that supports a text label or stands in for one. Decorative icons are hidden from screen readers; an icon that carries meaning on its own needs a text alternative.

Usage Notes

  • Hide decorative icons with aria-hidden="true" so screen readers don't announce them next to a visible label. Older guidance also adds focusable="false" to the <svg>.
  • An icon that stands alone and carries meaning needs a text alternative (WCAG 1.1.1): role="img" with aria-label, or Visually hidden text. In an icon-only Button, give the name to the button, not the icon.
  • Don't rely on an icon's shape or color alone to convey status; pair it with text (WCAG 1.4.1 Use of Color).
  • Icons that convey information need a contrast ratio of at least 3:1 against their background (WCAG 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast).
  • Use fill="currentColor" and size icons in em so they follow the color and size of the text around them.
