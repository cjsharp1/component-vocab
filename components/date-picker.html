Date picker

A date field with a button that opens a calendar for choosing a day. Use it when the date is close to today or the weekday matters, such as booking a stay; people should still be able to type the date.

Also known as
Calendar, Datetime picker. In design systems: DatePicker (Ant Design); Date picker (Carbon, Polaris); Date time picker (Atlassian); Date Picker (Chakra UI, shadcn/ui); Pickers (Apple HIG)
Semantic element
<input> and <button> opening a <dialog> with a date grid

Usage Notes

  • Choose a Date picker for dates near today, such as appointments or bookings, where seeing the weekday helps. For birth dates and other known dates, a Date input is faster.
  • Always let people type the date. The calendar is a helper, and a typed field is easier for many keyboard and screen reader users.
  • Follow the APG Date Picker Dialog example: the button opens a dialog, arrow keys move through the grid of days, Escape closes it and focus returns to the button.
  • Give each day an accessible name with the full date, not just the number, and mark today and the selected day in a way that doesn't rely on color alone (WCAG 1.4.1).
  • Show unavailable dates as disabled rather than hiding them, and tell people why a date can't be chosen when the reason isn't obvious.
