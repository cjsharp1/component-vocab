Switch

An on/off control for a setting that takes effect as soon as it is flipped, such as email notifications. If the choice is only applied when a form is submitted, use a Checkbox.

Also known as
Toggle, Lightswitch. In design systems: Switch (MUI, Ant Design, Polaris, Chakra UI, Radix Themes, shadcn/ui, Fluent UI); Toggle (Carbon, Atlassian); Toggles (Apple HIG)

Usage Notes

  • Use a Switch for a setting that applies the moment it's flipped. In a form that is saved with a submit button, use a Checkbox.
  • Add role="switch" to a native checkbox so screen readers announce on and off instead of checked. The checkbox still handles focus, Space and form submission.
  • Label the setting, not the action: "Email notifications", not "Turn on email notifications". The label must not change between states.
  • Don't show the state by color alone (WCAG 1.4.1). The thumb position carries it, and the track and thumb need 3:1 contrast against their surroundings (WCAG 1.4.11).
  • For a button that stays pressed, such as Bold in an editor, use a Toggle button.
