Usage Notes

  • Use for small user identity markers in comments, chat, lists, or attribution lines. For full person display with name and bio, use Profile.
  • Provide fallback content when no image is available: initials from the user's name, a generic icon, or a placeholder.
  • Status indicators (online/offline/busy) are typically small dots placed at the bottom-right of the avatar.
  • In avatar groups, use overlapping stacks to save space or a row layout for clarity.
