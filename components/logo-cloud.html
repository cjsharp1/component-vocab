Logo cloud

A collection of partner, client, or technology logos arranged in a row or grid. Communicates trust and social proof through recognizable brand marks.

Layout Variations

Usage Notes

  • Use for showing client/partner logos on a landing page. Keep logos at consistent heights. Grayscale treatment prevents visual competition with page content.
  • Normalize logo sizes by height (not width) to maintain visual balance.
  • Grayscale or monochrome treatment creates a cohesive look and prevents color clashes.
  • Include alt text for each logo for accessibility.
  • For scrolling/animated logos, use the scrolling variant (formerly Marquee).
