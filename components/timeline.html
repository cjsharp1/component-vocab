Timeline

A chronological sequence of events, milestones, or process steps. Displays items along a time axis with dates, titles, and descriptions.

Usage Notes

  • Use for company history, project milestones, or process steps. For step-by-step progress indicators, use Steps. Timeline shows past/planned events; Steps show progress through a process.
  • Always present events in chronological order (oldest to newest or newest to oldest).
  • Use semantic <ol> for ordered list of events with proper list item markup.
  • Alternating layout works well for detailed events; left-aligned is better for compact timelines.
