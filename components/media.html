Media

Pairs an image or video with adjacent text content. A fundamental pattern for content that needs visual accompaniment with explanatory text side by side.

Usage Notes

  • Use for content that pairs text and image side by side. For full-width image with caption below, use Figure. For image-led previews, use Card.
  • Ensure vertical alignment looks balanced — center alignment often works best.
  • On mobile, Media typically stacks vertically with the image on top.
  • For videos, include play controls and consider autoplay policies.
