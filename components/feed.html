Feed

A scrolling list of articles that loads more as the user reaches the end, such as a news or social stream. The ARIA feed role lets screen reader users move from article to article while new ones load.

Usage Notes

  • Use role="feed" only for a list of articles that grows as the user scrolls. A fixed set of posts is a List, or a Grid of Cards.
  • Make each <article> focusable and label it, usually with its heading. Set aria-posinset and aria-setsize (-1 when the total is unknown), and aria-busy="true" on the feed while articles are added.
  • The ARIA Authoring Practices give feeds Page Down and Page Up to move between articles, and Control+End and Control+Home to move focus past the feed, so keyboard users are not trapped in it.
  • Endless loading puts the footer out of reach and makes it hard to return to a position. Consider a Load more button, or Pagination when people need to come back to a specific place.
