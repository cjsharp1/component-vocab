Popover

A floating panel of rich content triggered by a click. More complex than a Tooltip — can contain headings, images, links, or forms.

Also known as
In design systems: Popover (MUI, Ant Design, Carbon, Polaris, Chakra UI, Radix Themes, shadcn/ui, Fluent UI); Popovers (Bootstrap, Apple HIG); Popup (Atlassian)

Usage Notes

  • Use for supplemental content that is richer than a Tooltip allows. For simple text hints, use Tooltip.
  • For action lists, use Menu button. Popover is for informational or form content.
  • Dismiss on outside click or Escape key. Optionally provide a close button for clarity.
  • Position automatically to stay in viewport, or allow manual positioning.
