Card

A contained preview of a piece of content. Presents an image, title, excerpt, and link as a clickable unit that leads to full content elsewhere.

Also known as
Tile. In design systems: Card (MUI, Bootstrap, Ant Design, Chakra UI, Radix Themes, shadcn/ui, Fluent UI); Tile (Carbon)
Semantic element
<article> or <div>
Native HTML
<article> inside a list

Usage Notes

  • Use Card for teasers that link elsewhere. For in-place content, use Feature. For people, use the person variant (formerly Profile).
  • When the entire card is clickable, ensure the hit area is clear with hover states.
  • Maintain consistent card heights in grid layouts by setting a max-height or line clamp on excerpts.
  • For image-less cards, consider using a subtle border or shadow to define card boundaries.
  • For people, use a Card with an Avatar (person variant) rather than a separate Profile component.
