Usage Notes

  • Use for customer quotes and reviews. For general blockquotes within body text, use Quote. For pull-quotes, use Quote.
  • Always wrap testimonial content in <blockquote> for semantic HTML.
  • Keep quotes concise — aim for 2-4 sentences. Long testimonials lose impact.
  • Include attribution context (role/company) to add credibility to the quote.
