Article

A self-contained, independently distributable piece of content. Blog posts, news items, forum posts, or product cards that could stand alone outside the page.

Usage Notes

  • Use for blog posts, news, or any content that has a publication date and author.
  • Wrap article body text in the Prose component.
  • Each Article should have its own heading (h1 or h2 depending on context).
