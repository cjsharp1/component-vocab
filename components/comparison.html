Comparison

Side-by-side comparison of two or more items. Products, plans, features, or before/after states presented for easy evaluation.

Usage Notes

  • Use for feature comparison tables or product comparison. For pricing specifically, use Pricing. For general tabular data, use Table.
  • Highlight the recommended or most popular option to guide user choice.
  • Use checkmarks (✓) for included features, dashes (—) or empty cells for excluded features.
  • Keep row labels short and scannable. Users should quickly understand differences.
