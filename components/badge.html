Badge

A small indicator attached to another element — showing a count, status, or "new" marker. Draws attention to changes or unread items.

Also known as

Usage Notes

  • Use for notification counts, unread indicators, or status dots attached to buttons, icons, or avatars.
  • For counts over 99, consider truncating to "99+" to keep the badge compact.
  • Ensure color choices meet accessibility contrast requirements against both the badge background and the page/anchor element.
  • A dot badge is useful when you want to indicate presence or activity without a specific count.
