Toggle button

A button that stays pressed or not pressed, such as Bold in a text editor or Mute in a video player. For a setting that is on or off, use a Switch.

Also known as
In design systems: Toggle Button (MUI); Toggle (shadcn/ui)

Usage Notes

  • Keep the label the same in both states and let aria-pressed carry the state. If the label changes, as with Play and Pause, drop aria-pressed; the ARIA Authoring Practices advise against changing both.
  • Use a Toggle button for a pressed state in an editor or player. For a setting that is on or off, use a Switch. For one choice among several, use a Segmented control.
  • Show the pressed state with more than color, such as a filled background plus a border (WCAG 1.4.1, 1.4.11).
  • Icon-only toggle buttons need an accessible name, such as aria-label="Bold".
  • In a group, each toggle button keeps its own aria-pressed and can be pressed independently.
