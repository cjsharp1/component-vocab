Drawer

A panel that slides in from a screen edge, either overlaying content or pushing it aside. Used for navigation, filters, detail panels, or forms.

Also known as
Offcanvas (Bootstrap), Side Sheet (Material), Drawer (Ant), Sheet (Apple), Sheet/Drawer (shadcn)
Semantic element
<div> with appropriate ARIA

Usage Notes

  • Use for mobile nav, filter panels, or detail views that need more space than a Popover.
  • For blocking actions that require full attention, use Modal instead. For persistent sidebars, use Aside.
  • The "Overlay" behavior places the drawer above content. "Push" behavior shifts the main content aside.
  • Always provide a way to dismiss: close button, backdrop click, or Escape key.
