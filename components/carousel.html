Carousel

A paginated slideshow cycling through cards, images, or content panels. Shows one or a few items at a time with navigation.

Also known as

Usage Notes

  • Use when space is limited and you want to show multiple items in sequence. For all-visible collections, use Gallery or Card List.
  • Avoid auto-play unless absolutely necessary. Auto-advancing carousels can be disorienting and inaccessible.
  • Provide clear navigation: arrows, dots, or swipe gestures on touch devices.
  • Ensure keyboard navigation is supported: arrow keys to navigate slides, Tab to focus controls.
