Progress

A visual indicator of task or process completion. Shows how far along a process is.

Also known as
Progress (Bootstrap), Linear/Circular Progress (Material), Progress (Ant), Progress View (Apple), Progress (shadcn)
Semantic element
<progress> or <div> with role="progressbar"
Category

Usage Notes

  • Use when completion percentage is known. For indeterminate loading, use Spinner.
  • Linear bars are best for horizontal layouts. Circular rings work well for compact spaces.
  • Steps are ideal for multi-stage processes like forms or onboarding.
  • Ensure the progress indicator is labeled with aria-valuenow, aria-valuemin, and aria-valuemax for accessibility.
