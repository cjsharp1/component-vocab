Container

Constrains content to a maximum width and centers it horizontally within the viewport. Acts as a wrapper to prevent content from stretching edge-to-edge on wide screens.

Usage Notes

  • Nest content components (Card, Feature, etc.) inside Container.
  • Do not nest Container inside Container.
